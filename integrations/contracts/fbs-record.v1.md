# Shared records, v1

How FBS apps keep shared records the same (owner 2026-11-18: any connected app may edit a shared field, and the change reaches every other connected app). Plan: `mcp-fbs-suites/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-18-every-app-two-way-sync.md`. Template: `templates/connector/`. Tokens: `mcp-fbs-suites/docs/contracts/fbs-connector-token.v1.md`. Rules 7, 18, 21, 33, 46, 47.

Built for `person`, 2026-11-18; `holiday`, `branch` and `leave_type` 2026-11-20 (below) (personal fields, government ids, bank, gap fill and `department` 2026-11-19: People, Pay, Learn): FBS People (the center), FBS Pay (with salary), FBS Learn (staff), FBS Work (workspace members), FBS Stock (store owners) and FBS Connect (staff accounts, never the client portal). The other types follow the plan.

## Send changes

`PUT {app base_url}/api/fbs/connector/changes`, `Authorization: Bearer <connector token>` (aud = the receiving app, `tenant` = its tenant, `scope` = `connector.{type}.write`, single-use `jti`).

```json
{"changes": [{
  "change_id": "01JD…", "type": "person", "external_id": "people:12:345",
  "origin": {"app": "people", "external_id": "people:12:345"},
  "changed_by": {"app": "pay", "first_name": "Pia", "last_name": "Lim"},
  "changed_at": "2026-11-18T06:12:30.123456Z",
  "fields": {"mobile": "0917…", "department": "Finance"},
  "match": {"email": "ana@acme.test", "first_name": "Ana", "last_name": "Cruz"}
}]}
```

- At most 500 changes. Only the fields that changed. Money as 2-decimal strings in major units.
- Answer `{success: true, message: "", data: {answers: {change_id: answer}}}`. The answer is one of:
  - `accepted`;
  - `unchanged`: that change id was taken before;
  - `superseded`: an equal or later change of each field is already here;
  - `held`: no such record here; it waits for a person to link it;
  - `refused`: a bad shape, or a type this app does not take;
  - `locked`: inside a finalized pay period, or a suggestion already decided in the app (`work_item`), or a billable row already on an invoice (`money_item` credit_sale / billable_hours).
- Refusals of the whole call: 401 `bad_token` / `replayed`, 403 `scope`, 404 `no_tenant`, 422 `bad_request`.

## Rules every app follows

- **The later change wins, per field.** Each app keeps when each shared field of a record was last set (`changed_at`, `change_id`). A change is taken for a field only when it is later. A tie goes to the type's center.
- **Loop guard.** An app sends only changes its own people or jobs made, never one it took. Wrap every save another connector makes from another app's data as "received".
- **Centers.** People for `person` and `time`, Connect for `customer` and `money_item`, Stock for `product`, Work for `work_item`, Pay for `cost_rate`. The center passes a change it took on to every other linked app, under its own id, never back to the app that made it. A non-center app sends to the center when the org has it linked, else to every linked app that takes the type.
- **Ids.** Once the center has a record, its id is the shared id (`people:{company}:{employee}`). An app finds a record by that id, or by the id it sent the record under.
- **Auto-link (spec §0).** Every `person` change carries `match`: who the person is. A receiver that does not know the id links it to the one person it has with the same email and the same first and last name (case aside), and keeps that id. People keeps any app's id (`app_fbs_links`); the other apps link only People's ids (`people:…`). Two matches, or a different name, stay held.
- **Login emails (owner 2026-11-18 option 2).** In an app whose people are sign-in accounts (Work, Stock, Connect), an `email` another app changed is never applied at once: it waits for that app's admin (the change answers `held`). Confirm applies and audits it; Reject keeps the email and marks the change `superseded` there. Names and the other fields are taken as usual. A `status` from another app never switches off the last active admin or owner.
- **One approval (rule 73).** A change approved in the app that made it arrives approved: the receiver writes it as approved and never asks again. Only a change nobody approved yet (`approved` false, such as FBS Work's hours) gets its one approval in the receiver.
- **Never merged, never deleted.** A record an app lacks, with no clear match, is held for its people. A delete is sent as `status: inactive`.
- **Linked apps.** Ask `GET {FBS Suites}/api/fbs-id/connector-peers?tenant=` before each run. A change waits until every app it goes to has answered; the next run sends it again.

## Fixtures (plan gap G15)

A type's fields are pinned by a JSON schema kept in `mcp-fbs-suites/docs/contracts/` and copied byte for byte into each side's `tests/Fixtures/contracts/`: the sender checks what it records against `$defs`, the receiver accepts every `examples` entry. Change the schema there first, then both copies in the same work. So far: `learn-people-school.v1.json` (`evaluation`, `benefit`; FBS Learn → FBS People, 2026-11-21), `pay-work-cost.v1.json` (`cost_rate`; FBS Pay → FBS Work), `shared-lists.v1.json` (`branch`, `leave_type`; both ways between FBS People and FBS Pay, Learn's leave types not yet), `departments.v1.json` (`department`; People, Pay and Learn), `holidays.v1.json` (`holiday`; People ⇄ Pay, People → Learn and Work; a change carries the changed fields plus `date` and `type`) and `stock-connect-product.v1.json` (`product`; Stock → Connect; Stock now sends `price` as a 2-decimal string, as the money rule above says, and Connect takes either), all 2026-11-21. People, Pay and Learn check with one `contractErrors($file, $def, $fields)` test helper. The other types are still pinned only by each side's own tests.

## Filling gaps (owner 2026-11-19)

A field one app holds and another lacks (a birth date entered only in People, a department typed only in Learn) is filled in the other app, never overwriting what it has.

- A change may carry `"fill": true`. The receiver writes each field of it only where its own value is empty (null or empty text), and never stamps that field's time, so any real change later still wins. It answers `accepted` when it wrote a field, else `superseded`. A fill for a record the receiver lacks is `superseded`, never held. The center passes a fill it took on (`accepted` or `superseded`) to the other linked apps, still marked `fill`.
- Each app runs `php artisan fbs-sync:fill-gaps` every night (scheduled, one server, no overlap), per tenant with FBS ID on and its `link` switch on: one `fill` change per person it shares (People: every employee; another app: the people it holds under a People id), with only the shared fields it holds that are not empty, and only when they differ from that person's last fill change (nothing new = nothing sent). It also records a `department` change for each active department it has never sent (below). Salary is never in a fill.

## Departments (built 2026-11-19, owner: a new department in one app shows in the others)

Type `department`, People the center, between People, Pay and Learn (`SyncTypes`). One record per department: People's `people:{company}:department:{id}`; Pay's `pay:{company}:department:{name}`; Learn's `learn:{school}:department:{name}` (the name in lower case, single spaces). Fields: `name`, `status` (`active` / `inactive`), and `previous_name` on a rename.

- Sent when an app's own people add, rename, archive or restore a department (Learn: also when a staff member is saved with a department name it has never sent), and by the nightly fill for departments never sent.
- The receiver finds the department by name (case and spacing aside). A rename (`previous_name`) renames the one with the old name when none has the new name yet; any other unknown name is added, active (Pay: under its oldest active branch; with no branch, `refused`). `status: inactive` archives it when it exists; nothing is ever deleted, and people keep their department.
- Never two departments of one name in an app. A change id already taken is `unchanged`; an equal state is `superseded`. No field times: departments are matched by name, and the last arrival wins.
- The loop guard and forwarding are those of `person`: a department saved from another app's change is never sent back, and People passes each one it took to the other linked apps.
- An app's own Department pages and pickers list the shared ones like any other.

## Holidays and shared lists (built 2026-11-20, FBS Connected Data phase 1)

Spec: `mcp-fbs-suites/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-connected-data-phase-1.md`. FBS People is the center of all three (owner decision 22). Each is kept once: an app with the same list sends its own changes to People and takes People's, which People passes on under its own id (`people:{company}:{type}:{id}`). The receiver keeps the sender's id against its own row (the change's `match.id`), so later changes find it; a first change is matched by date and type (holiday), by code then name (leave type) or by name (branch). Later change wins; a change no later than the last one taken for that row is `superseded`. Nothing is deleted: `status: inactive` switches the row off (a received holiday is removed in People, archived in Pay and Learn).

| Type | Apps | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `holiday` | People ⇄ Pay, People → Learn, People → Work (shown on People) | `date` (Y-m-d), `name`, `type` (`regular`, `special_non_working`, `special_working`), `status`. Company-wide holidays only: a branch's or an entity's own holiday, Pay's `double_holiday` and People's holidays made by the FBS Learn connector ("No classes") stay in their app. Learn keeps them as published Holiday events, never touches an event the school made, makes none for a day the school already marked or for a special working day, and sends none. |
| `branch` | People ⇄ Pay | `name`, `address`, `status`. Pay refuses a new branch over its plan's one-branch limit. |
| `leave_type` | People ⇄ Pay, People → Learn | `code`, `name`, `paid`, `days_per_year`, `status`. Company-wide types only (not an entity's own). Pay's leave types have no code or off switch (matched by name; `inactive` changes nothing there). Learn keeps them as leave categories. |

## Tally (nightly check, Task 6)

The type's center (People for `person`) checks every night that the linked apps hold the same shared fields, without any value crossing (rule 18).

`POST {peer base_url}/api/fbs/connector/tally`, a hub connector token with scope `connector.person.read` (same checks as `changes`: aud, tenant, single-use jti), body `{"type": "person", "salt": "<32 to 64 hex, new each run>"}`. 422 for another type or a bad salt, 403 for another scope.

```json
{"success": true, "message": "", "data": {"records": {"people:12:345": {"first_name": "9c1f…16 hex", "last_name": "…", "status": "…"}}, "truncated": false}}
```

- One entry per record another app knows (Learn: every staff member under `people_external_id`, else its own id; Pay: employees with an `external_id`; Work, Stock, Connect: their synced accounts), at most 5000.
- Per field it holds of `first_name`, `middle_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `mobile`, `employee_number`, `department`, `position`, `date_hired`, `status`: the first 16 hex of HMAC-SHA256 of the value with the salt, after lower case, trimming and single spaces; `status` is `active` or `inactive`; null is the empty string. `employment_type` is left out (each app words it its own way).
- The asker finds each id with its own `PersonType::find`, hashes its own fields the same way, and keeps only the ids and field names that differ, plus how many ids it could not find (People: `app_fbs_tallies`, `sync_status`). It reports the counts to the hub with its sync health (`connector-report` `peers.*.tally`), never the ids' values. Nothing is changed by a tally.

## Settings › FBS apps (built 2026-11-18: Work, Stock, Connect)

Each app has one admin page listing its linked apps (`connector-peers`; when the hub does not answer, the apps it sent to in the last 30 days), the types each way, the last answered send, changes waiting, the last failed send (kept 30 days in the app's cache, cleared by the next answered send) and the Waiting for you holds. Counts, times and type names only, never field values (rule 18).

AI hold decisions: every app with holds offers `propose_hold_decision` (a proposal only, key ability `mcp.propose`; an admin confirms in the app). Pay grants `mcp.propose` per fbs-id-token.v1 step 5 (2026-11-19).
