# Client data ownership, extraction and retention

The policy on who owns the data in FBS Suites, what a client gets back when a subscription ends, and when that data is deleted.

- **Applies to:** every FBS app sold as part of FBS Suites (People, Pay, Connect, Work, Stock, Learn, the CRM, FCA-SMS) and every new project.
- **Related system rules:** 7 (multi-tenant subscription products: tenant states, export while suspended, deletion only after a stated retention period), 18 (privacy by design), 27 (import and export).
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-12

The policy text below is the owner's wording. Change it only with the owner's approval.

## 1. Data ownership and access during active subscription

Clients retain their rights and ownership over the original information and records they encode, upload, submit, or maintain through FBS Suites ("Client Data").

During an active subscription, clients may access, manage, and download their Client Data, as well as view and utilize system-generated reports, computations, summaries, analytics, dashboards, and other outputs available through their subscribed applications.

Clients are responsible for regularly downloading and maintaining independent copies of important business records necessary for their operational, administrative, financial, regulatory, and statutory requirements.

## 2. Data extraction upon subscription termination

Upon termination, expiration, or discontinuation of a subscription, clients may request an electronic copy of their original Client Data directly encoded, uploaded, or submitted into FBS Suites.

Standard data extraction shall be limited to the original client-provided information in an available, commonly used electronic format, such as CSV or Excel, depending on the nature of the data and the technical capabilities of the application.

System-generated reports, computations, summaries, analytics, dashboards, processed outputs, visualizations, and other information produced through the application's automated functionalities shall not automatically be included in the standard data extraction package. These outputs are generated through FBS Suites' proprietary application processes, business logic, and functionalities.

However, this limitation shall not prevent clients or authorized data subjects from obtaining records that they are legally entitled to access or are required to maintain under applicable Philippine laws and regulations.

## 3. Data extraction period and procedure

Clients shall be given thirty (30) calendar days from the effective date of subscription termination to request and retrieve their eligible Client Data.

Extraction requests must be submitted through FBS's designated support or administrative channels and shall be subject to verification of the requesting party's identity and authority.

FBS shall provide reasonable assistance in extracting eligible Client Data in its available standard formats. Requests involving customized data conversion, specialized extraction, or additional technical services may be subject to separate assessment, applicable service fees, and mutual agreement.

## 4. Data retention and secure deletion

After the thirty (30)-day extraction period, FBS may proceed with the secure deletion or disposal of the client's data from its active systems, subject to applicable legal retention requirements, pending obligations, and other lawful grounds for continued storage.

FBS may retain information only when necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, or legitimate legal purposes. Any retained personal data shall continue to be protected in accordance with applicable data privacy and security requirements.

Clients remain responsible for maintaining their own copies of business and operational records required under applicable Philippine laws and regulations.

## 5. Data privacy and regulatory compliance

Nothing in this policy shall limit or prejudice the rights of data subjects under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), including applicable rights to access and data portability.

FBS and its clients shall fulfill their respective responsibilities as Personal Information Processor and Personal Information Controller, as applicable, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

Clients are responsible for ensuring the accuracy, lawful collection, and appropriate use of information submitted into FBS Suites. FBS shall implement appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect information processed through its services.

## 6. Intellectual property and proprietary system components

FBS retains ownership of its software, source code, database structures, system architecture, algorithms, templates, application logic, workflows, proprietary functionalities, and processes used in generating, processing, and managing information within FBS Suites.

The client's ownership and rights over its original Client Data shall remain unaffected by this provision. However, such rights shall not automatically extend to FBS's underlying software, proprietary functionalities, or intellectual property used to process and generate information within the applications.

## 7. Client responsibility for data preservation

Clients are strongly encouraged to regularly export, download, and maintain independent copies of important business and operational records throughout their active subscription.

Such records may include, but are not limited to:

- **Business and Customer Records:** Customer profiles, contact information, transactions, communications, and business-related records.
- **Financial and Accounting Records:** Financial transactions, accounting entries, invoices, receipts, and other supporting documents.
- **Payroll and Employment Records:** Employee information, compensation records, payslips, employment history, and statutory contribution records.
- **Inventory and Sales Records:** Product information, stock records, inventory movements, purchase transactions, and sales records.
- **Educational and Academic Records:** Student information, enrollment records, academic records, attendance, and other school-related information.
- **Operational and Administrative Records:** Project information, tasks, schedules, facility bookings, service requests, and other operational records.

FBS shall not be responsible for the client's failure to preserve or maintain copies of records that the client is legally required to retain, provided that FBS has fulfilled its applicable contractual and legal obligations concerning data access, processing, and extraction.
